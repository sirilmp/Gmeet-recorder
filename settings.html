Settings

Changes save automatically

Meet page

Floating control bar
Shows on the Meet tab while recording, with Stop and Bookmark. Drag it anywhere, or pop it out to keep it on top of every tab.
Pop the bar out when I leave the meeting tab
While recording, switching to another tab or app moves the bar into a small window that stays on top, so Bookmark and Stop are always at hand. It goes back when you return to Meet.

Google Drive

Upload after every recording
Uses your connected Drive. Goes to Meet Recordings → date folder.

Recording

When I join a meeting
Chrome only lets an extension start recording after a click or keypress from you, so this can't be fully automatic. “Remind me” shows a prompt as soon as you join, and one keypress starts it. Recording stops by itself when you leave.
Default quality
Used when you open the popup. You can still change it per recording.

Transcript

Transcribe every recording
Starts by itself once a recording is saved and runs in the background, one recording at a time, even with the library closed. It pauses while you record.
Speech model
Runs on this PC, no audio is sent anywhere. The model downloads once, then works offline. Bigger = more accurate but slower.
Spoken language
Auto-detect works for most meetings. Pick one if it guesses wrong.
